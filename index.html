How vetted grades a dev

Every grade comes from public on-chain data. vetted watches every pump.fun launch live, records how each one turned out after its first hour, and keeps that record per dev wallet. It never forgets.

WHAT COUNTS

  • Dev dumps. The dev sold most of their bag within the coin's first hour. -15 each.
  • Dead coins. Past launches that never got off the curve. -8 each.
  • Serial launching. Three or more launches in 24 hours. -15.
  • This coin. Dev already sold (-30), dev holds 20%+ (-10), 10%+ bought by other wallets in the launch block (-15).
  • Graduations. Past launches that made it to the DEX. +15 each.

Everyone starts at 60 (C). A is 80+, B 65+, C 50+, D 35+, F below 35. A fresh wallet has no record, which is why the board also shows what the dev is doing on this coin right now.

ANTI-VAMP

vetted refuses to launch a coin whose ticker or name matches any pump.fun launch from the last hour. Copies launched elsewhere still show up on the board, but the original came first.

YOUR KEYS, YOUR COINS

Launches and trades are built by PumpPortal and signed by your own wallet. vetted never holds keys or funds and charges nothing. Memecoins are risky; a good grade is a record, not a promise.
