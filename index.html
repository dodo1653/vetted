    how vetted grades a dev

    every grade comes from public on-chain data. vetted watches every pump.fun launch live, records how each one turned out after its first hour, and keeps that record per dev wallet. it never forgets.

    • dev dumps: the dev sold most of their bag in the coin's first hour. -15 each
    • dead coins: past launches that never got off the curve. -8 each
    • serial launching: three or more launches in 24 hours. -15
    • this coin: dev already sold -30, dev holds 20%+ -10, 10%+ bought by other wallets in the launch block -15
    • graduations: past launches that reached the DEX. +15 each

    everyone starts at 60 (C). A is 80+, B 65+, C 50+, D 35+, F below 35. a fresh wallet has no record, which is why every card also shows what the dev is doing on that coin right now.

    anti-vamp

    vetted refuses a launch whose ticker or name matches any pump.fun launch from the last hour. the original came first.

    your keys, your coins

    launches and trades are built by PumpPortal and signed by your own wallet. vetted never holds keys or funds and charges nothing. a good grade is a record, not a promise.
